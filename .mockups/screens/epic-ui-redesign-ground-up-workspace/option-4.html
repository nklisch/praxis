Chain rule — nested rates

The rate of the whole is the product of the rates when changes nest. Don't say "cancel" — say "multiply." The notation hides a story about rates compounding.

For the balloon: dV/dt = (dV/dr) · (dr/dt). The geometry rate (4πr²) times the physics rate (2 cm/s) gives 200π cm³/s. Two separate rates, one product.
