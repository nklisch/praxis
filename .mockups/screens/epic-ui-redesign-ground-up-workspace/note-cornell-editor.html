◆ Notes

The rate of the whole is the product of the rates when changes nest. Don't say "cancel" — say "multiply." The notation hides a story about rates compounding.

When two changes compose — when the position depends on time but the velocity also varies, when the price depends on supply but supply depends on weather — the rate of the whole is the product of the rates.

For the balloon: volume responds to radius; radius responds to time. Two rates, one nesting.

dV/dt = (dV/dr) · (dr/dt) = 4πr² · (dr/dt)

At r = 5, dr/dt = 2: dV/dt = 4π(25)(2) = 200π cm³/s ≈ 628.3. The geometry rate (4πr²) times the physics rate (2 cm/s) gives the volume rate.

¶ Summary
The chain rule is just "rates multiply when changes nest." The formula (f ∘ g)' = f'(g) · g' is one breath of that sentence. The balloon example makes the multiplication concrete: geometry's rate × physics's rate = the rate of the whole.
