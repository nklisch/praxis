Exactly — and that's the geometry piece. dV/dr = 4πr² at r = 5 gives 100π. Now we just need to multiply by the rate at which r itself changes, which the problem hands you: 2 cm/s.

So dV/dt = (4πr²) · (dr/dt) = (100π)(2) = 200π ≈ 628.3 cm³/s. Two rates, one product — that's the chain rule with names attached. Let me make this more vivid with a second case.
