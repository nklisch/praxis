Locked palette · Option 3

Studio Quiet

Brutally minimal and architectural. Warm off-white surface with a true near-black, a single muted brick accent used sparingly, and mode markers reduced to a 7px color dot in the kicker rather than washes of color. The product almost disappears into its content — which is exactly the point for a tutor that refuses to compete with the student's focus.

Considered and rejected

  • Option 1 — Editorial Refined: warmer parchment + terracotta, refinement of the current direction. Rejected for being too close to where Praxis already is — the redesign earns its keep by moving farther.
  • Option 2 — Library Twilight: deep midnight indigo + burnished gold nocturne. Beautiful but committed to dark-first; the chosen Studio Quiet works equally well in either mode and reads as more architectural than nostalgic.
  • Option 4 — Cream & Indigo (remix): indigo nocturne + cream paper + copper accent. Visually striking; rejected because the indigo carries warmth and personality Studio Quiet deliberately strips out.
  • Option 5 — Field Guide: vintage botanical / Peterson's-guide. Wildly distinctive; rejected as the wrong genre — naturalist observation isn't the posture Praxis claims; quiet architectural restraint is.

Full alternative options visible in git history at commit prior to lock-in.
