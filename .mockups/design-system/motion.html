About this showcase

The locked motion contract for Praxis. Attitude: Productive — snap-to-final easing, 160ms quick transitions, no designed pause. The kinetic voice matches Studio Quiet's anti-notification stance: motion gets out of the way; the tutor's words are what hold attention. Downstream code links this file and references var(--ease-standard) / var(--dur-quick) / var(--hold-beat) by name; production CSS modules adopt these tokens during the migration sweep feature.

Durations · the Doherty budget

Three duration tokens, three interaction classes. Input-gating motion (any transition the user is waiting on) lives in --dur-instant or --dur-quick — Doherty < 300ms ceiling. Ambient motion (background, peripheral, indeterminate) is the only thing allowed in --dur-ambient.

Modal entry · hold-beat

The designed pause matters most here. In Calm-tech mode, the modal arrives, then a 250ms hold-beat passes in stillness, then the focus ring appears on the input. In Productive mode, focus appears immediately — no pause. Press "Open modal" and watch.

Chat streaming · per-chunk fade-in (no cursor)

Chat-text streaming uses .chat-turn__streaming-tail — a span that wraps each new chunk of arriving text and fades it from opacity: 0 → 1 via the chat-fade-in keyframe at --dur-ambient (480ms) and --ease-standard. The persistent "in-flight" signal stays on the speaker line via the pulsing dot in .chat-turn__streaming — the text body itself carries no perpetual animation. Cursors / blinking carets were removed 2026-05-24 as part of the chat-UX overhaul; the per-chunk fade conveys "this just arrived" without the terminal-style chrome.

Click play to re-trigger the fade. The badge on the speaker line is the persistent signal — it pulses while streaming, vanishes when settled.
