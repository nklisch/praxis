In the prompt configuration screen, the teaching-style section currently sits at the bottom of the form. It should move to the top — teaching style is the highest-signal, highest-frequency knob the user touches, and burying it under less-used options forces unnecessary scrolling to reach the primary lever. Likely a position re-order in the configurator panel layout; may also want to revisit the broader ordering so frequency-of-use drives placement rather than implementation order.
