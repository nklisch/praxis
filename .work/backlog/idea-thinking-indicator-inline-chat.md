The "thinking" indicators currently render outside the chat transcript — above and below it — rather than inline as part of the conversation flow. They should appear inline in the chat at the point where the model is mid-turn, so the transcript reads as a continuous stream and the indicator stays anchored to the message it belongs to instead of floating around the chrome.
