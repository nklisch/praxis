When the tutor poses multiple structured user-questions in one turn, the current display stacks them vertically and they fill the entire screen, forcing a lot of scrolling and burying the chat flow underneath. Switch to a tabbed (or pager-style) display where each question shows one at a time and the user advances to the next via tab/next controls, keeping the question surface compact regardless of how many questions are in the set.
