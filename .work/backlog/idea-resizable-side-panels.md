Make the side panels (sidebar / documents pane / workspace rail) user-resizable via drag handles, instead of fixed widths. Students and authors have different content density needs — a wider documents sidebar helps during reading-heavy sessions, while a narrow one maximizes chat space during back-and-forth tutoring. Should remember the chosen width per panel across sessions (probably via `config_kv` or localStorage), and respect min/max bounds to prevent unusable layouts.
