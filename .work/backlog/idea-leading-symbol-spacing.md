created: 2026-05-24
tags: [ui]

Leading symbols / icons that sit immediately before a word — in route headers, on the create-course screen, and elsewhere across the app — are rendering too close to the word they precede. The gap between the glyph and its label needs to be widened consistently so the symbol reads as a leading mark rather than crashing into the text. Audit the editorial primitives and any inline symbol-plus-label patterns project-wide and apply a uniform spacing token.
