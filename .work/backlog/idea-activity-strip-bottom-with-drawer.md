The ambient activity display (currently `<StatusStrip>` mounted under `<TopNav>` at the top of the app) should move to the bottom of the window, be slightly taller / more present, and gain a bottom-anchored drawer that can slide up to reveal a richer streamed feed of in-progress work (ingestion, indexing, drafter, grading, etc.). The collapsed bottom strip stays low-attention; the drawer is where a user goes when they want to see what the agents are actually doing in detail.
