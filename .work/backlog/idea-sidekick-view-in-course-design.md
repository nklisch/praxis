The sidekick view (the per-mode right-hand panel surfacing tutor scratchwork / live artifacts) may not fit course-design mode the way it does in study modes — the authoring flow has its own dominant surface (units, lessons, assessment plan) and the sidekick might be redundant or confusing here. Worth evaluating whether course-design should hide the sidekick, swap it for a draft-state inspector, or keep it as-is.
