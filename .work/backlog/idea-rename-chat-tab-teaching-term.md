Rename the "chat" tab to something teaching-flavored that better reflects what it actually is in Praxis — a tutoring session, not a generic chat. Candidate names: "Tutor," "Teacher," "Lesson," "Session," "Learn." The current "chat" framing borrows from generic LLM products and undersells the pedagogical role of the surface; a teaching-shaped name would better match the rest of the product language (lessons, gates, mastery, assignments). Final name choice deferred to scope time; this park is about making the change, not picking the word yet.
