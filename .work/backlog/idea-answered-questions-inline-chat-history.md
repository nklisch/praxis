Answered questions currently appear anchored just above the chat input rather than within the chat transcript where they were originally asked. They should be folded back into the chat history at the point where the question was posed, so the conversation reads chronologically and prior answers stay in context. The "anchored above input" treatment makes sense for an active / pending question, but once answered it belongs back in the scroll.
