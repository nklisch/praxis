The stop button in the course-design chat is in the wrong spot — it sits below the text input rather than in a natural action zone, and it doesn't visually read as a button. Worth re-designing: rethink placement (likely co-located with the send affordance or replacing it while a turn is in flight), give it a clear button treatment (shape, hit area, affordance), and confirm the same pattern works across all chat surfaces, not just course design.
